# GenAI-For-Data-Engineers
Gen AI Guide For Data Domain- This guide is designed to help data professionals to understand and leverage Generative AI techniques within the data domain. This guide provides practical insights, workflows, and best practices tailored for real-world data scenarios.


What’s Inside
* Overview of Generative AI: Key concepts, terminology, and the evolution of Gen AI in the data landscape.
* Tokenization & Embeddings: Step-by-step labs and explanations on tokenization, word2vec, and embedding techniques.
* Practical Notebooks: Hands-on Jupyter notebooks to experiment with tokenization, word2vec, and embeddings.
* Transformers Architecture: Understand Encoder-Decoder model architecture
* RAG and Vector Stores: Build a strong foundation with the modern AI databases.


Artificial Intelligence (AI):  is the ability of computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, and recognizing images. Instead of following hard-coded rules for every single action, AI systems process large amounts of data, find patterns, and make predictions or decisions.

Language AI : combines Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to understand, interpret, generate, and translate human languages

Natural Language Processing (NLP): is a subfield of artificial intelligence (AI) that combines computer science, computational linguistics, and machine learning to help computers understand, interpret, and generate human language.
